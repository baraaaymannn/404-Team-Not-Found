// System Overview
🏪 Clothing Store Management System
A complete management system for a clothing store that handles sales, inventory, customer data, staff management, and reporting. The system serves three main actors and coordinates multiple processes to ensure smooth store operations.
PROCESS SALE MANAGE INVENTORY GENERATE REPORTS MANAGE STAFF CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
// System Actors
🛍️
Customer
Browses inventory, requests out-of-stock items, provides feedback, and initiates purchases through the system.
🏷️
Cashier
Processes sales, handles returns, updates inventory, views customer history, and processes staff purchases.
👔
Manager
Manages inventory, generates reports, sets promotions, manages staff accounts, and performs system administration.
// Diagram 1 — Use Case
🛒
CUSTOMER UC
Browse & Request
Customer browses inventory via kiosk or app, can request out-of-stock items, and provides feedback about products.
💳
CASHIER UC
Process & Manage
Cashier processes sales, handles returns, updates inventory records, views customer purchase history, and processes staff purchases.
📊
MANAGER UC
Control & Report
Manager manages full inventory, generates sales and profit reports, sets store promotions, manages staff accounts, and performs system admin.
🔗
RELATIONSHIPS
Include & Extend
«include»: Process Sale always includes Make Payment. «extend»: Authorize Credit Card extends Process Sale only when payment method is credit.
// Diagram 2 — DFD Level 1
🔄
PROCESS 1
Process Sale
Receives purchase details from Customer. Writes sales data to D2, updates inventory in D1 and D4, and stores customer info in D3.
📦
PROCESS 2
Manage Inventory
Reads product/inventory data from D1. Sends inventory data to Generate Reports. Connected to the Vendor system through D4.
📋
PROCESS 3
Generate Reports
Receives data from Manage Inventory and Customer Management. Sends reports to Manager and receives/sends reports with Bank.
👥
PROCESS 4
Customer Management
Receives purchase details from Vendor. Reads and writes to D3 (Customer DB). Sends customer data to Generate Reports.
// Data Stores (D1–D4)
📁 System Data Stores
D1
Inventory DB
Stores all product stock levels and inventory records.
D2
Sales DB
Records all completed sales transactions.
D3
Customer DB
Stores customer profiles and purchase history.
D4
Vendor DB
Stores supplier/vendor information and purchase orders.
// Diagram 3 — Activity (Swim Lanes)
🛍️
CUSTOMER LANE
Approaches & Selects
Customer approaches counter with items and selects their payment method (Credit Card or Cash) when asked by cashier.
🏷️
CASHIER LANE
Greets & Processes
Greets customer → scans all items → asks for payment method → swipes/inserts card if credit → gives receipt and bags items.
💻
SYSTEM LANE
Calculates & Authorizes
Looks up item → displays price → calculates subtotal → connects to payment gateway → deducts payment → records sale → updates inventory → prints receipt.
⚡
SYNC BARS
Synchronization
Two synchronization bars coordinate parallel activities: after all items scanned, and after payment is processed before giving receipt.
// Diagram 4 — Ranking Matrix
🏆
RANK 1
Process Sale — HIGH
Highest business value and complexity. Depends on the Payment System. Must be implemented first as it is the core revenue function.
📦
RANK 2
Manage Inventory — HIGH
High business value, medium complexity, low technical risk. Depends on Vendor System. Critical for stock control and reordering.
🔍
RANK 3
Browse Inventory — HIGH
High business value, low-to-medium complexity, no dependencies. Easy to implement, high customer-facing impact.
📊
RANK 4
Generate Reports — M-H
Medium-high value and complexity. Depends on D1, D2, D3 data stores being populated first. Important for business decisions.
